The one that is not a list. The collections stand like books on a shelf, each a spine with its name set vertically and its count at the foot. The one you point at (or tab to) pulls out on the nav's spring and turns sage, as the tile plate and the nav do, with its tally and newest entries. At rest it is almost all type and air; open, it is the most information on the page. New collections add a spine. Costs: vertical text is slower to read; on touch there is no hover, so a tap would open it first and a second tap follow the link; a long shelf (more than eight) wraps to a second shelf.
