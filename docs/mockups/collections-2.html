The preview is the content. Each collection is a short table of its own newest rows (the data-table style the collection pages already use) under a section label. It has no tiles and no big numbers, so it cannot read as a dashboard; it reads as the contents page of a journal. The cost is height: four blocks fill two screens, and the order is fixed by me.

Since 2005 I have flown 161,324 miles in 123 flights to 12 countries. Since 2019 I have identified 214 birds in 58 families, and I keep 128 recommendations for friends, plus 41 Lego sets I have not yet written down.
