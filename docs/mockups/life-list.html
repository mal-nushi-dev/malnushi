      What this page decides

      • The map counts species, once in each place it was seen. The pills change that to observations or checklists. Zero is gray; one species is already a clear tint; the deepest color is the most in the current view, so opening the United States rescales to its busiest state.
      • The table moves to /log as Travels did. This page is the view: numbers, map, years, places, latest.
      • Privacy is the policy file. No location names, times or coordinates reach this page. A place reads as its county; on the map a point would be a 0.1° cell.

      Asked of this mock

      • Sparse export, mostly gray. With 19 species the map is nearly all gray; switch to the fuller sample to see 60. Is a straight line from 1 to the most right at that scale, or is a square-root step wanted later?
      • Major regions are drafted. Membership is my reading of eBird's lists and is not checked. Only regions with at least one species are shown; the empty ones collapse into a count.
      • Checklists are one bird each while the export is casual sightings. The list is built to read well when a checklist holds many.
      • Media is your own photographs matched by species, plus Macaulay Library numbers as link-outs once the export carries them. None does yet.
      • Not drawn: the globe view, the cells for exact places, counties, and the mobile layout.
